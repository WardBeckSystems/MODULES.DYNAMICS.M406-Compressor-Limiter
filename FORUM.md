> I'm actually reverse engineering the M406 right now. What I have at the moment is the pinout:
>  - 1 Input + (for hi level signals)
>  - 2 Input - (for hi level signals)
>  - 3 Input + (for lo level signals)
>  - 4 Input - (for lo level signals)
>  - 5 thru 12 no connection
>  - 13 Output +
>  - 14 Output -
>  - 15 Meter out +
>  - 16 Meter out -
>  - 17 n/c
>  - 18 0 volts audio
>  - 19 0 volts LED and chassis
>  - 20 Lamp V+ (+24 volts)
>  - 21 n/c
>  - 22 Audio V+ (+24 volts)

> Hello.
>
> Size is not an issue in either case. The axial caps will be smaller in diameter and shorter in length - that's progress. As far as the radial ones (with the leads coming from the same said on the can) spacing is not crucial. As technology has progressed the caps have become smaller, thus the leads (centre to centre) are closer together. With a little teflon tubing you can stretch the new leads to go further apart. It is advisable to have one lead un-molested, and the other lead will be bent (re-formed) and encapsulated with teflon tubing to span the distance to the new holes in the PCB. Common practice in prototyping.
>
> Tony - are you experimenting with Nichicon, Panasonic, Philips or any to that business to get "the proper capacitor sound"????? We used Philips because that was what was available, and WIMA when we could get them.
